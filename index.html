Questions

Does Jobwisp submit applications for me?

No. Jobwisp fills in the form, and you always review it and press Submit yourself. Anything it couldn't answer is marked "needs you," so nothing slips through.

Which job sites does it work on?

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and most company career pages, including application forms embedded in other websites. Some custom dropdowns, for example on Workday, may need a quick manual pick.

Is my information safe?

Your profile and resume are stored only in your browser. Jobwisp doesn't use AI, doesn't upload your resume and doesn't sell data. Read the full privacy policy.

What counts as one of my 3 free applications?

Each different application you fill counts once per day. Filling the same application again, or using the practice form, doesn't count. Your free applications reset at midnight.
