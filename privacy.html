Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

The short version: Jobwisp keeps your profile and resume in your own browser. We don't run servers that store your information, we don't use AI, we don't sell data, and we don't show ads. Jobwisp only types your information into the job applications you choose to fill, and you always press Submit yourself.

Who we are

Jobwisp is a Chrome extension that fills job applications, suggests jobs that match your resume, and tracks the applications you fill. In this policy, "Jobwisp," "we" and "us" mean the developer of the Jobwisp extension. You can contact us at YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

Information stored in your browser

The information you give Jobwisp is saved using Chrome's built-in extension storage on your device. It includes:

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, address, online profile links, work history, education, answers to common application questions, optional equal-opportunity answers, and saved answers you write.
  • Your resume file, if you add one, so Jobwisp can attach it to applications.
  • Your applications list: the title, web address and date of applications you fill, and the status you set.
  • Job Radar settings and saved jobs: the job titles, location and work types you search for, jobs you save, and companies you add.
  • Settings and setup progress, and your subscription status.

This information stays on your computer. It is not sent to us, and we can't see it.

Daily usage count: to count your free applications, Jobwisp keeps the web addresses of the applications you filled today. This count is saved in Chrome's sync storage, so if you've turned on Chrome Sync, Google syncs it between your signed-in Chrome browsers. It's reset each day.

Your resume scan

When you scan your resume, the file is read entirely inside your browser using built-in code. Your resume is not uploaded to us or to any other service, and no AI is used.

When information leaves your browser

Job application websites you choose

When you click Autofill, Jobwisp types your information into the form on the page you're viewing. That website receives your information only when you submit the application, and its own privacy policy applies from then on.

Job Radar

Job Radar loads public job listings directly from employers' job boards hosted by Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby. These requests ask for a company's list of open jobs; they don't include your profile, resume, job titles or location. Matching jobs to your job titles and location happens inside your browser. As with visiting any website, those services can see your device's IP address. The LinkedIn, Indeed, Google Jobs and ZipRecruiter buttons open a search on those sites with your job title and location, and those sites' privacy policies apply.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), with card payments processed by Stripe. If you subscribe, ExtensionPay receives your email address and payment status so it can confirm your subscription, and Stripe receives your payment details. Jobwisp never sees or stores your card number. To keep track of your subscription, ExtensionPay saves an identifier in your browser's extension storage and checks your subscription status with its servers. Please see the privacy policies of ExtensionPay and Stripe for how they handle your information.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't use your information for advertising, credit decisions or anything unrelated to Jobwisp's features.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools in the extension.
  • We don't submit applications for you.

Why Jobwisp asks for its permissions

  • Access to websites: needed to fill application forms on whichever job site you choose, including forms embedded from another site, and to load public job listings for Job Radar. Jobwisp only reads and fills a page when you ask it to.
  • Storage: to save your profile, resume and settings in your browser.
  • Scripting: to run the autofill on the page you're viewing, only when you click Autofill, press the shortcut or choose Apply with Jobwisp.
  • Extra storage space: so your resume file (up to 10 MB) and profile can be saved in your browser.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can view and edit everything in your Jobwisp profile at any time, and remove any detail you don't want to use.
  • You can export your profile to a file and import it on another computer.
  • Removing Jobwisp from Chrome deletes the information it stored in your browser.
  • To manage or cancel a subscription, use Manage subscription in Jobwisp. To ask about information held by our payment provider, contact us and we'll help.

Security

Your information is protected by Chrome's extension storage, which other websites and extensions can't read. Because it's stored on your device, please keep your computer and Chrome account secure.

Children

Jobwisp is meant for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Jobwisp's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use information only to provide and improve Jobwisp's features that you can see and use.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled in an important way, we'll let you know before it takes effect, for example in the extension or on this website.
