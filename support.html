How do I get started?

After you add Jobwisp to Chrome, a welcome page opens. Click Get started, drop in your resume (PDF or Word), and Jobwisp fills in your profile. Check the details, answer a few quick questions, and you're ready.

Pin Jobwisp to your toolbar so it's always one click away: click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Jobwisp.

How do I fill an application?

Open the application form, click the Jobwisp icon and choose Autofill, or press Alt + Shift + F. A panel shows every field that was filled. Answer anything marked "needs you," check everything, then press Submit on the job site.

A field didn't fill. What can I do?

First, make sure that detail is in your profile (click the Jobwisp icon, then Profile). For questions that are specific to a company, add a Saved answer: type a few words from the question and your answer, and Jobwisp will fill it next time.

Some sites use custom dropdowns (Workday is a common example) that need a quick manual pick. If a form has room for fewer jobs than you've saved, click the form's "Add another" button, then choose Fill again.

The scanner got something wrong on my resume

The scanner reads your resume with rules, not AI, so unusual layouts can trip it up. Everything it finds is shown to you to check, and you can edit any detail in your profile. Resumes that are scanned images have no text to read, so try a PDF saved from Word or Google Docs instead.

How does Job Radar find jobs?

Job Radar checks the public job boards of companies that use Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, and shows openings that match your job titles and location. You can add a company by pasting its careers link (for example jobs.lever.co/company). For even more jobs, use the LinkedIn, Indeed and Google Jobs buttons on the Job Radar page.

How does the free plan work?

You can fill 3 different applications a day for free, with every feature included. Filling the same application again, or using the practice form, doesn't count. Your free applications reset at midnight on your computer's clock.

How do I subscribe, cancel or update my card?

Click the Jobwisp icon. To subscribe, choose Upgrade. To cancel or change your card, choose Manage subscription. If you cancel, Unlimited stays on until the end of the month you paid for. Payments are handled by Stripe through our payment provider, ExtensionPay.

I subscribed on another computer

Install Jobwisp, open the upgrade page and choose Already subscribed? Log in, then use the email you subscribed with.

How do I move my profile to another computer?

Open your profile and click Export to save a file. On the other computer, open your Jobwisp profile and click Import. Your profile is stored only in your browser, so it doesn't move on its own.

How do I delete my data?

Your information is stored in your browser. Removing Jobwisp from Chrome (in chrome://extensions) deletes it. See the privacy policy for details.
